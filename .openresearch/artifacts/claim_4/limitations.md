# Limitations and deviations

- The result falsifies Theorem 1.6 only under its literal unrestricted-`p`
  quantifiers.
- If the intended theorem assumes fixed `p in (0,1)` as `n,m` grow, the
  cardinality counterexample does not apply. That repaired theorem needs a
  separate proof or experiment and is not claimed false here.
- No Monte Carlo sample or optimizer is needed: the contradiction holds for
  every graph realization along the counterexample sequence.
- The paper's proof also appears to swap `epsilon p` and `p/epsilon` thresholds;
  this audit does not rely on that possible proof error.
