# Limitations — independent Claim 6 replication

- This verifies the paper's qualitative empirical pattern under both
  interpretations supported by its text. It does not claim pixel-for-pixel
  regeneration of the unpublished author run.
- Conditioning Bernoulli sets on non-emptiness is explicit. The paper does
  not define the maximum of an empty random set.
- SLSQP supplies a reproducible numerical optimum, not a symbolic global
  optimum certificate. Symmetry-reduced starts, disjoint validation,
  multi-seed Monte Carlo, and an independent checker reduce this risk.
- The final negative-correlation second differences are close enough to zero
  that uncertainty is essential. No violation is promoted unless its
  four-standard-error lower endpoint is positive.
- The protected 10/12 revision remains intact for Claims 1-5; this evidence is
  additive and target-only.
