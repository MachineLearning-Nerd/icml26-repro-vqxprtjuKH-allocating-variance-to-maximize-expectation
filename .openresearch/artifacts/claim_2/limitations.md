# Limitations and deviations — Claim 2

- This is a falsification of the literal theorem, not a failed reproduction.
- It relies on the explicit mismatch between the input definition `N(mu,Sigma)`
  and theorem output `N(0,Sigma_hat)`.
- It does not falsify a repaired zero-mean theorem. The negative control
  verifies that the same `n=2` construction is not a counterexample after that
  repair.
- No conclusion is drawn from the paper's separate `epsilon^3` versus
  `epsilon^8.5` grid inconsistency; the mean counterexample is sufficient.
