# Limitations and deviations — Claim 6

- No unreported `m`, seed, sample count, optimizer, or tolerance is invented.
- Pixel digitization verifies what the published image displays; it cannot
  recover uncertainty or prove that an independent run agrees.
- The proof route covers the independent concavity theorem, not the correlated
  Monte Carlo curves.
- The potential three-pixel negative-correlation anomaly is not called a
  falsification because it is not robust to line width and missing raw values.
